# Presentation Outline — cloud.txt Receipt Portal

## Slide-by-Slide Outline

### Slide 1: Title & Purpose
- **Heading**: cloud.txt Receipt Generator
- **Sub-heading**: Serverless Invoicing and Real-Time Business Cockpit
- **Details**: Built using Angular SPA & Google Apps Script.

### Slide 2: Problem Statement
- Small businesses lack integrated, cost-free invoicing utilities.
- Commercial invoice tools are expensive and store data in proprietary silos.

### Slide 3: Solution Architecture
- A dark-themed Angular application.
- Uses a private Google Sheet as a database and Google Drive for PDF storage.

### Slide 4: Real-time Invoicing Form
- Live item addition/removal.
- Automatic GST (18%) and Grand Total calculation via Angular Signals.

### Slide 5: Analytics Dashboard
- Interactive charts showing monthly trends and cash vs. online revenue splits.
- Top-selling products ranking.\n
